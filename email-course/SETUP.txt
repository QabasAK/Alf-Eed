1000 Hands · الف ايد · FREE SIGN COURSE BY EMAIL

A spaced-repetition JSL course. Every subscriber starts at lesson 1 the day they sign up.
8 lessons teach 3 signs each (24 signs). Every sign comes back for review 1, 3 and 7 weeks
after it is taught. 12 emails over 15 weeks, then it ends by itself.

It costs nothing to run. It lives in the liu.thousandhands@gmail.com Google account: a Google
Form for sign-ups, a Google Sheet that remembers who got what, and a small Apps Script that sends
the emails from that Gmail address. No subscriber cap, no domain, no paid plan. The only limit:
a free Gmail account can send about 100 script emails a day, and the script stays under that. If
more people are due on one day, the rest get their email the next day.

WHAT'S IN HERE

  signs.csv          The signs. Open in Excel or Google Sheets. One row per sign.
                     Fill in video_url, creator (their name) and creator_url (their Instagram).
                     The sign names are suggestions; change anything.
  lessons.csv        One Deaf culture note per lesson. Write them with, or have them checked
                     by, a Deaf advisor.
  build_course.py    Turns the files into the course. Run:  python build_course.py
  sender.gs          The sending logic (you don't need to touch it).
  course-plan.html   The whole schedule, plus a list of everything still missing.
  emails/            A preview of every email. Open them in a browser.
  apps-script/Code.gs   The finished script to paste into Google (the course + sender.gs).

BEFORE YOU LAUNCH

  1. Ask 2–3 Deaf creators or interpreters for permission to link their videos. Link to their
     original post; never download, cut or re-upload their clips.
  2. Fill in signs.csv and lessons.csv, run build_course.py, and check course-plan.html says
     "Nothing" is missing.
  3. Have a Deaf advisor look over the emails once before anything goes out.

SETTING IT UP (once, about 20 minutes, signed in as liu.thousandhands@gmail.com)

  1. Google Forms → new form "Learn JSL with 1000 Hands".
     Settings → Responses → Collect email addresses → "Responder input" (so anyone can type
     their email, without signing in to Google). You don't need any other questions.
  2. In the form: Responses → "Link to Sheets" → create a new spreadsheet.
  3. In that spreadsheet: Extensions → Apps Script. Delete what's there, paste ALL of
     apps-script/Code.gs, and click Save.
  4. Choose "setup" in the function menu at the top and click Run.
     Google will ask you to authorise it, and may warn "Google hasn't verified this app".
     That's normal for your own script: Advanced → "Go to … (unsafe)" → Allow.
     This creates the Progress tab and makes the script run every morning and after each sign-up.
  5. Make the unsubscribe link work: Deploy → New deployment → type "Web app" →
     Execute as "Me", Who has access "Anyone" → Deploy. (Until you do this, the unsubscribe
     link opens an email to you instead.)
  6. Test: choose "sendTestToMe" and Run. All 12 emails arrive in the inbox. Check the
     links and how it looks on your phone.
  7. Share the form's link (Send → link icon) on Instagram and, later, on the website.

  The Progress tab shows every subscriber, how many emails they've had, and if they unsubscribed.
  Don't edit or reorder it by hand.

CHANGING THE COURSE LATER

  Edit signs.csv or lessons.csv, run build_course.py, and paste the new apps-script/Code.gs over
  the old one in Apps Script (Save; no need to run setup again). People already on the course
  carry on from where they are with the new content. To add lessons, add rows with lesson 9,
  10, ...; the review emails at the end move automatically.

  Tip: ask subscribers to reply "hi" to the first email or add the address to their contacts,
  so later lessons don't land in spam.
