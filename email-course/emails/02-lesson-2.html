DEAF CULTURE  ثقافة الصم
Your face is part of the sign. In sign languages, eyebrows, head movements and mouth shapes carry grammar, not just mood. A head shake can turn a sentence into "no", and the same hands with a different face can mean something else. So don't keep a blank face when you practise. Copy the signer's face too.
