DEAF CULTURE  ثقافة الصم
Getting someone's attention: a Deaf person can't hear you call their name, so tap them lightly on the shoulder, wave where they can see you, or flick the lights in a room. Then keep eye contact. Looking away while someone signs is like walking off mid-sentence.
