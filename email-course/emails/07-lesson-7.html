DEAF CULTURE  ثقافة الصم
When you study or work with a Deaf person through an interpreter, talk to the Deaf person, not the interpreter. Look at them and say "you", not "tell him" or "tell her". In group work, sit in a circle so everyone can see everyone's hands and faces, and take turns instead of talking over each other.
