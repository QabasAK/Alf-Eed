# الف ايد field kit

This folder is for a team building a sign language lexicon for one academic field.
`PLAYBOOK.md` describes the process and `templates/terms.csv` is the source of truth for terms.

## Non-negotiable

- Never invent, describe, choose or approve a sign. Signs come from Deaf signers, and the
  sign columns in `terms.csv` are theirs to fill in.
- Mark every Arabic definition you draft as `needs_review` until a subject expert has read it.
- Never state a fact, partnership, student count or result the team hasn't given you.
  Leave a clearly marked gap instead.
