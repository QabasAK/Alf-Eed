# University pitch

Fill in `templates/university_one_pager.md` for one specific reader.

1. Ask who's reading it and what they can actually say yes to. An accessibility office can
   link the lexicon, an instructor can use it in a course, and a dean can fund it.
2. Lead with the single smallest ask that person can approve. Cut the other checkboxes.
3. Fill in the progress numbers from `templates/terms.csv` by counting rows: `sign_status`
   for signs and `animation_path` for videos, checking that the files exist.

## Don't make things up

Only use facts the team has given you. Don't invent student numbers, statistics, quotes,
partnerships or endorsements. Leave anything missing as a visible `[NEEDS: ...]`, and list
those gaps in your reply.

## Tone

Write as students proposing a concrete project, not a charity appeal. Don't describe Deaf
students as inspiring or as a burden. Keep it to one page, and offer a matching short email
in the language the reader uses, usually Arabic.
