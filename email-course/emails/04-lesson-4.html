DEAF CULTURE  ثقافة الصم
Jordanian Sign Language is a full language with its own grammar. It isn't Arabic on the hands, and it isn't universal: it's different from American Sign Language, and even neighbouring Arab countries sign differently. Questions show on the face too: watch the eyebrows in this week's videos.
