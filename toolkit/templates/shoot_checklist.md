# Shoot checklist

Session: ____________  Date: ____________  Signer(s): ____________

## Before the day

- [ ] Signers have the term list and definitions at least a few days ahead
- [ ] Signers have decided which sign to use for each term, and flagged any with no agreed sign
- [ ] Signers have given written consent covering where the videos will appear and for how long
- [ ] Credit and payment are agreed
- [ ] A sign language interpreter is booked if anyone on the crew doesn't sign

## Framing

- [ ] Plain, solid background that contrasts with the signer's skin and clothing
- [ ] Plain clothing with no patterns or logos, and nothing on the hands or wrists that distracts
- [ ] Head to waist in frame, with room for the arms at full reach
- [ ] Even light on the face and hands, because facial expression is part of the sign
- [ ] Camera on a tripod at chest height, shooting 1080p at 60 fps so fast movement stays sharp

## Each take

- [ ] Hold a resting position, sign once at natural speed, return to rest
- [ ] Signer picks the take they're happy with
- [ ] Log the take in `terms.csv`: `sign_video_path`, `sign_status`, `signer_credit`

## Afterwards

- [ ] Signers review the final edit before anything is published
- [ ] Exports are compressed for the web (H.264 MP4)
