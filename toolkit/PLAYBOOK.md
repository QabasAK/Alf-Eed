# The playbook

How to build a lexicon for your own field, based on the engineering one.

## 1. Bring Deaf people in first

Before picking terms, find the Deaf signers and Deaf students who will lead the signing
work. Agree how they'll be credited and paid. A lexicon designed without them and "checked"
at the end is the failure mode this whole initiative exists to avoid.

## 2. Pick your field and your number

Every field gets its own number, and it needs a true, nerdy reason. Engineering has 42
because of *The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy*. Use `/pick-your-number`.

The number is a joke, but it's also a real scope limit: a small fixed list is something a
small team can finish.

## 3. Choose the terms

Split the number into two to four categories, as evenly as it allows (engineering:
14 × 3). Choose what students meet first and use most, not what's most impressive. Fill in
`templates/terms.csv` with `/term-list`.

## 4. Write bilingual definitions

Two or three sentences each, in plain language. A subject expert reviews every Arabic
definition before it's used.

## 5. Film the signs

Deaf signers decide which sign is used, whether it's established or needs agreeing on.
Prepare each session with `/sign-shoot` and `templates/shoot_checklist.md`.

## 6. Make the concept videos

About 15 seconds each: What → Why → Where, with Arabic and English captions, built around
one everyday example. Use `/concept-video`.

**Check them with Deaf viewers early.** The captions have to carry everything, since these
videos have no narration.

## 7. Publish

Each term needs its name, both definitions, the sign and the animation, all searchable in
both languages. Video-heavy sites burn through bandwidth, so choose a host without bandwidth
charges (we use Cloudflare Pages).

## 8. Get a university to adopt it

Start with whoever already looks after Deaf students, then one instructor willing to use it
in one course. `/university-pitch` drafts the ask from `templates/university_one_pager.md`.

## 9. Tell us

When your field goes live, we'd love to add your number to the wall.
